# Code of conduct

Steelreed projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
including its enforcement guidelines.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment of any
kind is not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **conduct@steelreed.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
